# v0.4.1 Backfill Planner Acceptance Evidence — 2026-08-05

This record covers the REA-L-CH1 full-range inventory-planner correction. It
does not change precipitation-type science, FDB retrieval in a daily task, or
the monthly GRIB publication contract from v0.4.0.

## Root cause and containment

The v0.4.0 planner used nine full `fdb-utils list` scans over the complete
`20050101..20250831` range and buffered the union output. It therefore
enumerated large numbers of GRIB messages even though planning only needs the
set of available cycle dates. It also had no checkpoint. Job `5017247` timed
out on `pp-short` after 59m21s with 1,178,808 KiB peak RSS. Moving the same
operation to `pp-long` did not solve the algorithmic problem: job `5018505`
timed out after 2h00m11s with 1,304,664 KiB peak RSS and no manifest. The
dependent launchers `5018508` and `5018715` never ran; the redundant fallback
planner `5018714` was cancelled. No campaign, scratch, or `/store_new` product
path was created by these attempts.

## Index-query selection

Balfrin benchmarks used `fdb-list --compact --porcelain` for a full calendar
year of REA-L-CH1:

| Job | Query | Elapsed | Peak RSS | Result |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `5019248` | depth 3 | 2m49s | 428,508 KiB | completed |
| `5019255` | depth 2, one required field | 39s | 75,068 KiB | 365 dates |
| `5019273` | depth 2 plus sentinel step 24 | 40s | 74,256 KiB | 365 dates |
| `5019304` | all nine ICON fields, eight bounded workers, one year | 1m30s | 673,340 KiB | 365/365 dates, zero retries |

The accepted planner therefore splits the inclusive range by calendar year and
uses depth-2 probes for every required field. It selects step 24 for `T`, `P`,
`QV`, `TOT_PREC`, `T_G`, `RAIN_GSP`, `SNOW_GSP`, and `GRAU_GSP`, and step 0
for `HHL`. This is deliberately a date-availability sentinel, not a substitute
for exact completeness: each monthly task still retrieves and validates every
required step and model/surface level for each independent daily `0000` cycle.

Each completed year is written atomically to `manifest.inventory.json`.
Checkpoint schema and the complete query contract must match before reuse; a
malformed or out-of-range cached date fails visibly. A successful manifest and
Slurm-script publication removes the checkpoint. A failed or timed-out plan
retains it.

## Restart and full-range validation

Job `5019311` was deliberately limited to five minutes. Slurm terminated it
after 5m28s, and the atomic checkpoint retained four complete calendar years
(`2005..2008`) without a partial year. Job `5019318` resumed those years and
completed the same strict full-range ICON plan in 13m52s with 684,896 KiB peak
RSS. The two allocations consumed 19m20s in total, including the intentionally
discarded partial-year work at the first timeout.

The completed schema-v2 manifest selected all 7,548 requested dates, reported
zero missing cycles, 189 successful field probes, zero retries or exhausted
operations, and 248 monthly periods. Its first interval ends at
`2005-01-01 01 UTC`; its final interval ends at `2025-09-01 00 UTC`. The
checkpoint was removed only after both `manifest.json` and `manifest.sbatch`
were published. Planning created neither the configured scratch path nor the
configured `/store_new` product path.

## Operator and storage contract

`tools/submit_backfill_campaign.sh` is the accepted one-command path. It
submits the resumable planner to `pp-short` with eight CPUs. Only after strict
planning succeeds does the planner job submit the generated 248-element
`pp-long` monthly array. Re-running the same command resumes the checkpoint;
it does not bypass missing-date validation.

The established v0.4.0 January measurement remains applicable because the
daily science and GRIB writer are unchanged: 7,548 cycles project to about
415,953,795,168 bytes (`416 GB`, `0.38 TiB`) and about 40 ideal wall-hours at
eight concurrent monthly tasks. The current layout projects 248 monthly GRIB
archives plus `ARCHIVE_CONTRACT.json` on `/store_new`, 748 campaign-root files,
and 997 persistent files overall. At validation time `/store_new/mch` reported
239 TB available and 14,491,057 free inodes.

## Conclusion

The multi-hour behavior was a planner defect, not evidence that the 20-year
diagnostic workload itself was running. The corrected inventory plan is
bounded, observable, and resumable. Exact daily retrieval remains the final
completeness authority, so the optimization does not weaken the backfill's
scientific or publication checks.
