## Accumulation-difference correction

The January ICON comparison exposed a flaw in the previous negative-delta rule.
It found 3,559,836 negative `TOT_PREC` adjacent differences, none below
`-0.002 mm`. Treating each as a reset and substituting the full current
accumulator activated 1,904,701 otherwise inactive ICON columns. The same rule
also made 266,261 raw-active columns report an archived phase-component sum
above 150% of total precipitation; simply clamping negative component
differences to zero reduced that count to 16.

Live realtime audit job `5015583` (`pp-short`, completed in 27 seconds, exit
`0:0`) confirmed that this is not REA-specific. Between steps 1 and 2 of the
`20260805 1200` CH1 cycle, control/member 010 had 4,656/6,802 negative
`TOT_PREC` differences out of 1,147,980 grid points. Every decrease was exactly
or less negative than `-0.0009765625 mm`, yet the old reset substitution would
have assigned current accumulations as large as 35.408/33.514 mm to those
hours. The `20260805 0600` CH2 control had 621 negative differences out of
283,876, with a minimum of `-0.000030517578125 mm`; member 020 had none. No
sampled CH1 or CH2 decrease was below `-0.002 mm`, and the component fields
showed the same packing-scale behavior. The audit made 40 FDB retrieval/decode
attempts with zero retries or exhausted operations.

The corrected contract therefore applies to realtime and REA alike: an
accumulator must be monotonic inside its cycle, every negative decoded adjacent
difference is clamped to zero, and clamp counts remain visible in
`summary.json`. A decrease is never converted into the full current
accumulation. Corrected January ICON production and three-model candidate
acceptance are recorded below once completed from the exact release revision.

## Operational conclusion

Plain GRIB concatenation is valid and interoperable for this product. The
implementation performs the equivalent byte concatenation only after each day
passes the existing science and output checks, assigns one writer to a month,
validates ordering and D+1 semantics, calls `fsync`, and publishes atomically.
The schema-v2 layout is suitable for subset or full REA processing without
creating an unsafe number of files on `/store_new`.

Before launching all 20 years, operations still needs to confirm the current
long-term destination and retention owner. Schema-v1 manifests remain bound to
release `v0.3.0` and must be re-planned with v0.4.0.
